# Polat Sales Conversion — Package C

Repository-only synthetic extension to the existing Phase 9 Conversion Engine and accepted Polat conversation/response contracts. No new offer writer, invoice, genuine receipt, subscription consumer, sender or provider integration.

## Behavior

- Strict tenant/conversation-bound, versioned approved synthetic catalog snapshots reuse product/plan/knowledge validation, exact decimal price strings, published/effective/expiry checks, eligiblePlan, buildLeadIntelligence, buildConversion and commercialEvidence. Canonical snapshot hashes are evidence fingerprints, never approval or financial authority.
- The customer sees concise EN-default wording, or independently verified/currently requested TR wording. First-contact AI disclosure remains explicit; there is no Polat impersonation or fabricated familiarity. Social/identity turns delegate to the accepted conversation renderer instead of receiving unsolicited sales pitches.
- Prices and inclusions are exact cited commercial facts; ambiguous/multiple/expired/ineligible plans remain unresolved. Returning membership is only a customer self-report. Price/trust objections can be answered together. No discount, scarcity, profit guarantee, testimonial or personalized investment advice is invented.
- Purchase interest leads to review of plan details, not a claimed purchase or created invoice. Payment, VIP, refund, cancellation and exceptional negotiation require human review; no support action or manager notification is claimed completed. Payment, subscription and VIP status remain UNAVAILABLE without authoritative evidence.
- The sales renderer now feeds the existing fixture response pipeline through a strict whole-context binding. Existing QA and native takeover fencing remain in force. A held sales response creates only the existing synthetic human obligation; a safe answer can receive only an explicit fake acknowledgement.
- Requested follow-ups are bounded, exact-source/revision/idempotency contracts with consent scope/expiry, opt-out, contactability, takeover/open-case, complete-history, frequency, local-time window and manager-review checks. Even a qualifying preview retains dispatch=DISABLED. No scheduling job or campaign sender is registered. An opt-out acknowledgement does not falsely claim an account preference was saved.

- 75 focused sales domain tests, including 15 realistic invented dialogue cases plus negative catalog, language, authority, precision, opt-out and follow-up cases.
- Five sales→existing QA/ownership/fake transport integration cases.
- One actual local PostgreSQL 17 sales bridge: persisted current source → approved synthetic catalog → QA → guarded preparation → one fake effect → immutable fake acknowledgement. Zero public outbound messages and zero genuine receipts.
- Full unit/integration: 1746 PASS, 0 FAIL, 17 explicit DB-context skips. The actual sales DB bridge was executed separately; unrelated skipped tests are not reported as passed. Workspace lint and strict TypeScript PASS. Exact-head and post-merge mandatory CI/build remain publication gates.

Original local failures are retained outside Git. No QA assertion or financial gate was weakened.

## Limits

Real provider/model acceptance and opt-out/consent operational activation require separate scope. Existing Phase 10 classifier, Phase 11 consumer, Phase 14 privacy, Phase 15 metric definitions and LIVE gates remain unchanged. Overall Phase 16C-1 OPEN; commercial NO-GO.
